# InterceptLauncher
This is a gimbal-based device that launches ping pong balls at a specified angle. I was at a hackathon last weekend and we made a device called AstroShield, which tracks space debris coming at a satellite or spaceship and launches an intercept to knock it off course. InterceptLauncher is a prototype of how the aiming system for AstroShield would work.

It uses a gimbal system to be able to turn in a 3D space and launch an intercept at a piece of space debris at any angel. It has two stepper motors to turn it on the two axis, and one bearing to be a passive turner for the inner ring. I put a tube in the center as a placeholder for the actual launching system, which would most likely be a coilgun.
